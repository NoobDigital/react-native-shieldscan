# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to maintaining a professional, welcoming, and constructive open-source community where people can participate safely and respectfully.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Communicating with empathy, patience, and respect
- Welcoming questions and different levels of experience
- Giving and receiving specific, constructive technical feedback
- Focusing discussion on the work rather than the person
- Acknowledging mistakes and helping repair their impact
- Respecting privacy, boundaries, and confidential reports

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or sustained disruption
- Insults, personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately inflammatory comments
- Unwanted personal attention or repeated contact after a request to stop
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Deliberately submitting malicious code, unsafe instructions, or misleading security reports
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, community channels, and situations where someone officially represents the project.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. If the repository supports private vulnerability reporting, its private advisory form may be used to request a confidential contact channel. Do not publish sensitive incident details in a public issue.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and impartially as practical. Maintainers will protect the reporter's privacy to the extent reasonably possible, while recognizing that some information may need to be shared to investigate and resolve an incident.

## Enforcement

Depending on context, severity, and prior behavior, maintainers may:

1. Request a correction or edit
2. Issue a private or public warning
3. Temporarily restrict participation
4. Remove content or close an interaction
5. Permanently ban participation in project spaces

Retaliation against a person who makes a good-faith report is not acceptable. Deliberately false or abusive reports may themselves result in enforcement.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communications that conflict with this Code of Conduct. When appropriate, they will explain moderation decisions and apply standards consistently.

## Attribution

This project-specific policy is informed by common open-source community practices, including the Contributor Covenant. See https://www.contributor-covenant.org/.
